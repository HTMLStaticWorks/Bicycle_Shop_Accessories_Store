Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our gear, return policy, or special orders? Find your answers below.

What is your return policy on accessories?

We accept returns on all unused accessories in their original packaging within 30 days of purchase. Simply bring your receipt to the shop or contact us to initiate a return.

Can you special order a specific part or accessory?

Yes! If we don't have the specific component or accessory you're looking for in stock, we can special order it from all major brands. Special orders typically arrive within 3-5 business days.

Do you offer installation services for purchased parts?

Absolutely. Our professional workshop technicians can install any tires, chains, lights, or locks you purchase from us. Small installations can often be completed while you wait, while more complex parts may require booking a service.
